↑Bring back your last command

Copy and paste in Git Bash: right-click, or Ctrl Insert / Shift Insert.

Useful extensions

  • Live Server: opens your page and reloads it when you save. Right-click index.html → Open with Live Server.
  • Prettier: tidies your formatting when you save.
  • Live Share: lets your instructor see your code when you need help.
  • Code Spell Checker: catches typos in text and names.
